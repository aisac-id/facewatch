FaceWatch in 60 seconds

1 · Pick a source. Demo feed works instantly. Webcam is one permission away. For CCTV, use your camera's MJPEG stream URL (e.g. http://192.168.1.10:8080/video) or a JPEG snapshot URL polled every second. Optional Basic-auth user/pass supported. RTSP-only cameras need a tiny gateway (go2rtc, ffmpeg) that re-exposes MJPEG — browsers can't speak RTSP.

2 · Build the database. Press Enroll face to capture whoever is in frame, give them a name, done. Open a person to add more samples from the camera or upload photos — more samples, angles and lighting = far more reliable matches. Export/import the whole database as JSON.

3 · How matching works. Every face becomes a 128-number "face fingerprint" (descriptor). FaceWatch measures the euclidean distance between the live fingerprint and every enrolled sample; the closest person wins. Distance below the threshold (default 0.50) = recognized; above = UNKNOWN with an alert. Same person typically scores 0.25–0.45, different people 0.6+.

Keys: Space pause · E enroll · M mirror · S sound · ? help · Esc close.

🔒 Privacy: detection, recognition and the face database all run and live on this device (chrome.storage.local). The only network traffic is the camera stream you point it at. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
